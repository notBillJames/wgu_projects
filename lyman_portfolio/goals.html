One of my dreams is to work in baseball. I have been a fan of the game since I was a child, and I have always been fascinated by the business side of the sport. I would love to work for a Major League Baseball team, or even for the league itself. I would also love to work in the media side of baseball such as broadcasting or journalism. I have always been interested in the way that baseball is covered in the media, and I would love to be a part of that process.
